Kya laptop ya computer zaroori hai?+

Nahi. Kitaab mobile-first hai — har zaroori kaam phone se shuru ho sakta hai. Jahan computer thoda aasaan hota hai, wahan kitaab saaf bata deti hai, lekin zaroori nahi.

Kya coding aani chahiye?+

Bilkul nahi. Website bhi no-code tools se banana sikhaya gaya hai. AI-assisted coding wala chapter optional hai.

Kitaab kis bhasha mein hai?+

Roman Hindi mein — yaani Hindi, English letters mein (jaise "Aap kaise ho"). Technical shabdon ka simple matlab Glossary mein hai.

Payment ke baad eBook kaise milegi?+

Payment hote hi aapko PDF ka download link mil jayega. Koi dikkat ho to apni payment ID ke saath WhatsApp kijiye.

Kya mobile par padh sakte hain?+

Haan. Ye PDF hai — kisi bhi PDF reader ya Google Drive app mein khul jaati hai.

Kya isse kamaai ki guarantee hai?+

Nahi — aur jo guarantee de, uspar shak kijiye. Ye kitaab hunar, tareeke aur system sikhati hai. Nateeja aapki mehnat, market aur consistency par depend karta hai.

Isme kaunse AI tools use hote hain? Kya paise lagenge?+

Zyadatar kaam free plans se hota hai — Chat AI (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude jaise), Canva, Google Drive/Sheets, WhatsApp Business. Paid plan kab sochna hai, ye bhi kitaab batati hai.

Main bilkul beginner hoon. Kya samajh aayegi?+

Haan — kitaab zero se shuru hoti hai. Har chapter mein kahani, step-by-step process aur chhota practice task hai.

Refund policy kya hai?+

Digital product hone ki wajah se refund niyam hamari Refund Policy mein diye gaye hain. Kripya kharidne se pehle padh lijiye.

Support kaise milega?+
